========================================
LIBRARIAN SIDEBAR NAVIGATION - FIXED
========================================

ISSUE:
------
When logged in as librarian, clicking sidebar menu items just refreshed the page
and didn't navigate to the clicked pages (Books, Categories, Authors, etc.)

ROOT CAUSE:
-----------
All librarian pages had incorrect access control:
  requireRole('admin')  ❌ WRONG

This blocked librarian users from accessing their own pages, causing redirect back
to dashboard, which appeared as just a "refresh" to the user.

SOLUTION:
---------
Changed all librarian pages to use correct role:
  requireRole('librarian')  ✅ CORRECT

FIXED FILES:
------------
✅ views/librarian/books.php
✅ views/librarian/categories.php
✅ views/librarian/authors.php
